# Filing360

Product context for the design-first marketing site.

## Audience
Indian founders, CAs/consultants, NGO operators, and foreign/NRI teams seeking registrations and compliance.

## Promise
Full legal/compliance marketplace breadth with product-grade UI: curated mega-menu, honest `professional fee + government fees` pricing, cinematic hero, and conversion-focused lead forms.

## Non-goals (this phase)
Auth, payments, dashboards, MCA APIs, lead CRM, WhatsApp bots.

## Brand
**Filing360** — configured in `src/lib/siteConfig.ts` and CSS tokens in `globals.css`.
